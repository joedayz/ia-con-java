Burger Battle (board game) quick rules and reference

Objective
- Build and serve the best burgers to score points.

Classic Burger (reference recipe)
- Bun (top and bottom)
- Beef patty
- Cheddar cheese
- Lettuce
- Tomato
- Pickles
- Onion
- Ketchup and mustard

Gameplay (very short)
- On your turn, draft ingredients, then assemble burgers that match recipes.
- Completed burgers are scored and cleared from your tray.

FAQ
Q: What ingredients are on the Classic burger?
A: Bun, beef patty, cheddar, lettuce, tomato, pickles, onion, ketchup, mustard.
